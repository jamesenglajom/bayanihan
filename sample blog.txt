Introduction
Distance doesn't dampen the spirit of Kalayaan. While the Philippines enjoys the tropical heat of June, Filipinos in Sweden are embracing the long "White Nights" of the Nordic summer to celebrate our national identity. This year, the Bayanihan Exchange in Sweden (BES) is proud to highlight the various festivities happening across the country.

What’s Happening?
The Flag Raising Ceremony (Stockholm):
Join the community at the Philippine Embassy for the traditional morning assembly. There is something uniquely moving about hearing Lupang Hinirang echo through the streets of Stockholm.

Bayanihan Picnic & Salo-Salo:
We are moving the party to the parks! Expect a potluck featuring a fusion of flavors—think Chicken Adobo paired with Swedish Nypotatis (new potatoes). It’s the perfect example of our dual identity.

Cultural Showcase:
Local Filipino-Swedish dance troupes will be performing traditional folk dances. It’s a chance for the younger generation born in Sweden to connect with the rhythm of the Tinikling.

The Significance of 'Bayanihan' in the Diaspora
Celebrating Independence Day in a foreign land isn't just about history; it’s about the community we build here. In Sweden, "Bayanihan" takes on a new meaning—it’s how we support each other through the dark winters and how we integrate our vibrant culture into the Swedish way of life.

Join Us!
Whether you are a long-time resident or a newcomer to Sweden, we invite you to wear your best Barong or Filipiniana (or just something with the colors of the flag!) and join the festivities.
